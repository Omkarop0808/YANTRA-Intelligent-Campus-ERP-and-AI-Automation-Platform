This report presents competitive research and open-source project exploration to guide 
future roadmap improvements for **Yantra** (Intelligent Campus ERP & AI Automation Platform).

### Strategic Takeaways for Yantra:

1. **Early Risk & Dropout Prediction**: Highly sought-after feature across academic ERPs. Integrating ML predictive models with attendance trends will elevate Yantra's administrative value.
2. **Dedicated Parent/Guardian Portals**: The single most recurrent gap in developer-built ERPs. Offering role-based portals for guardians with direct alert feeds bridges campus-home communication.
